# Nanoha's Spells and Voice Lines

This document organizes the primary attack/binding magic used by Takamachi Nanoha in the "Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha" anime series, movies, and games, to serve as a reference for voice Mod selection.

## Main Moves

| Move Name | Voice Line | Move Description |
|---------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Divine Shooter** | "Shoot!"; "Accel!" during acceleration | One of Nanoha’s signature early homing spells. It first forms a Divine Sphere, then fires magic bullets that are trackable and can be controlled individually by Nanoha; it features fast casting and rapid fire, used to suppress high-speed targets and compensate for her own lack of mobility. Primarily seen in TV season 1. |
| **Accel Shooter** | "Accel Shooter!"; Game short version "Shoot!" / Charged version "Accel Shooter!" | A multi-shot homing shooter enhanced via the cartridge system from Divine Shooter. It allows simultaneous control of ten to dozens of magic bullets, serving purposes of suppression, encirclement, and interception; it has been a common mid-to-long-range move for Nanoha since “A's”. |
| **Divine Buster** | "Divine... Buster!"; Game short version "Buster!" | One of Nanoha's most iconic direct-fire cannon attacks. It forms a massive beam with high-density magic, possessing high power and strong barrier-piercing capabilities; it is more suitable as a regular main cannon than Starlight Breaker. Used in TV season 1, movies, and multiple games. |
| **Divine Buster Full Power** | “Divine Buster, Full Power!” | Wide-area release form of Divine Buster. Expands the radiation range without significantly reducing power; used in season 1, episode 9 to simultaneously seal six Jewel Seeds. |
| **Divine Buster Extension** | “Divine Buster Extension!” | An ultra-long-range enhanced version of Divine Buster appearing in “A's”. Assisted by Raising Heart Excellion for precision aiming, it combines range, projectile speed, accuracy, power, and barrier-piercing capabilities, making it suitable as a long-distance sniping voice line. |
| **Excellion Buster** | “Excellion Buster!”/“Break Shoot!” | A high-power main cannon used in Excellion/Exceed mode, allowing for limited ballistic control at medium range. The maximum output form is Force Burst, which uses Barrel Shot to fix the target followed by multiple shellings; represented by scenes in “A's” episodes 11 and 12. |
| **Excellion Buster A.C.S.**(Accelerate Charge System) | “Accel Charger, activate! Strike Frame!”→“Excellion Buster A.C.S., Drive!”→“Break Shoot!” after hit | A high-speed assault cannon. Nanoha forms a semi-solid magical blade with the Strike Frame, high-speed-piercing the opponent's barrier to release the Excellion Buster at zero distance inside the defense. It has extremely high power, but the user also endures the close-range-burst recoil and aftershocks; represented by the scene in “A's” episode 11. |
| **Barrel Shot** | — | A preparatory composite magic for Excellion Buster: simultaneously executes barrel stabilization, shockwave suppression, and invisible binding, fixing the target and establishing a stable ballistic path for the subsequent main cannon. In the anime, it appears more as part of the entire Excellion Buster process, with no confirmed instance of Nanoha shouting this as a standalone move name. |
| **Straight Buster** | "Straight Buster!" | A direct-application version of Excellion Buster that enhances the reaction explosion effect upon impact; explosions can chain-react between dense targets, achieving high-efficiency area annihilation with relatively economical magic consumption. Primarily seen in “StrikerS”. |
| **Restrict Lock** | Animation signature line "Got you!"; the game's charged version is also "Got you!" | A tracking-type binding magic that locks targets with rings/light bands. Nanoha often uses this to restrict enemies before following up with Divine Buster or Starlight Breaker; the finishing sequence against Vivio in episode 25 of “StrikerS” is particularly typical. |
| **Starlight Breaker** | TV season 1: "Try and take this, a variation of Divine Buster!", "This is my full power!", "Starlight... Breaker!"; Movie: "Try and take this! This is my full power!", "Starlight... Breaker!" | Nanoha's signature ultimate focused-beam artillery. It gathers scattered residual magical energy from the battlefield and releases it all at once; therefore, the more intense the battle and the more residual magic present, the greater its potential power; the drawback is the long-charging time, which usually requires the target to be bound first. |
| **Starlight Breaker Plus / ex** | Primarily still "Starlight Breaker!"; often accompanied by "Full power!" | An enhanced form of Starlight Breaker. `Plus` extends the charge time to increase output and adds barrier-breaking capabilities; `ex` uses cartridges to shorten preparation time and increase firepower, and is one of the "Triple Breakers" in the final battle of “A's”. |
| **Starlight Breaker ex Full Burst** | "Full power! Full power!!!", "Starlight... Breaker!", "Break... Shoot!" | An omnidirectional form used in episode 25 of “StrikerS”. It coordinates with four Blaster Bits to bombard from multiple directions simultaneously, using precision control to destroy the Relic inside Vivio's body; both the power and the equipment load are extreme. |
| **Hyperion Smasher** | "Hyperion Smasher!" | A high-power artillery shot exclusive to Full Drive in the PSP game “Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha A's PORTABLE -THE GEARS OF DESTINY-”. It has a slow activation, but the frontal light screen provides a guard window, and its single-shot damage is among the highest-tier in the game. |
| **Maneuver A.C.S.** | Usually consists of assault shouts; no unified move-name shouts confirmed | A close-range assault/grab action in the PSP game, utilizing the high-speed propulsion concept of A.C.S. It is not a new ultimate cannon, but is better suited as a reference for voice lines for gun-thrusts, uppercuts, or close-quarters burst actions. |
