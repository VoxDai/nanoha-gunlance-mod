# Monster Hunter: World and Iceborne Gunlance Moves

This list covers the Gunlance moves in Monster Hunter: World (MHW) and Monster Hunter World: Iceborne (MHWI) as a reference for mapping moves to game audio events.

## Ground Melee and Guard Moves

| Category | Move | Reference Name | Common Input/Activation Condition | Description | Version |
|------|--------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Draw | Lunging Upthrust (Draw Attack) | Lunging Upthrust (Draw Attack) | `Direction + Y` while sheathed | Moves forward and upward; same attack motion as the lunging upthrust while drawn | World |
| Thrust | Lateral Thrust I | Lateral Thrust I | `Y` | Basic horizontal thrust | World |
| Thrust | Lateral Thrust II | Lateral Thrust II | `Y` after Lateral Thrust I | Second stage of horizontal thrust | World |
| Thrust | Lateral Thrust III | Lateral Thrust III | `Y` after Lateral Thrust II | Third stage of horizontal thrust | World |
| Thrust | Guard Thrust I | Guard Thrust I | `Y` while guarding | Thrusts diagonally upward while keeping the shield in front | World |
| Thrust | Guard Thrust II | Guard Thrust II | `Y` after Guard Thrust I | Second stage of guard thrust | World |
| Thrust | Guard Thrust III | Guard Thrust III | `Y` after Guard Thrust II | Third stage of guard thrust | World |
| Thrust | Lunging Upthrust | Lunging Upthrust | `Direction + Y` | Steps forward and attacks from below upwards; a primary mobility attack | World |
| Slash | Rising Slash | Rising Slash | `Y + B` | An upward slash that can change the direction of subsequent shellings | World |
| Slash | Overhead Smash | Overhead Smash | `Y` after Rising Slash; or `Y + B` after certain moves | Heavy smash with the gun barrel; can derive into Full Burst or Wide Sweep | World |
| Slash | Wide Sweep | Wide Sweep | `Y` after Overhead Smash or Full Burst | Wide-range horizontal slash; can derive into Wyrmstake Cannon | World |
| Guard | Guard | Guard | Hold `RT` | Uses the shield to guard frontal attacks | World |
| Guard | Guard Movement | Guard Movement | Push left stick while guarding | Maintains guard while moving slowly | World |
| Guard | Guard Movement (Maintain Facing) | Guard Movement (Maintain Facing) | `LT + Direction` while guarding | Moves while maintaining guard direction; in “Iceborne”, `LT` also serves the purpose of Slinger aiming | World/Iceborne |

## Shelling, Wyrmstake, and Reloading Moves

| Category | Move | Reference Name | Common Input/Activation Condition | Description | Version |
|------|--------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|------|
| Shelling | Horizontal Shelling | Shelling (Horizontal) | `B` when muzzle is horizontal | Consumes 1 shell; can be fired twice consecutively | World |
| Shelling | Diagonal Shelling | Shelling (Diagonal) | `B` after Guard Thrust, etc. | Fires diagonally upward; can be fired twice consecutively | World |
| Shelling | Upward Shelling | Shelling (Upward) | `B` after Rising Slash, etc. | Fires straight upward; can be fired twice consecutively | World |
| Shelling | Charged Shelling | Charged Shelling | During combos that allow shelling, hold `B` | Fires after charging; can fire horizontally, diagonally, or upward depending on current muzzle state | World |
| Shelling | Full Burst | Full Burst | `B` after Overhead Smash | Fires all shells in the magazine at once; Normal type yields the highest benefit | World |
| Shelling | Wyrmstake Cannon | Wyrmstake Cannon | `B` after the second shelling; `Y` after Wide Sweep; `B` after Full Burst, etc. | Drives the Wyrmstake into the target, dealing continuous damage before exploding; requires a full reload to replenish the Wyrmstake | World |
| Shelling | Wyvern's Fire | Wyvern's Fire | `Y + B` (i.e., `RT + Y + B`) during Guard | Performs a three-stage powerful shelling after charging; enters cooldown after firing; direction can be adjusted during charging | World |
| Reload | Reload | Reload | `B` during Guard; or `B` after a step when ammo is not full | Refills shells and reloads the Wyrmstake | World |
| Reload | Quick Reload | Quick Reload | `RT + B` after attack combos | Refills normal shells, but does not replenish the Wyrmstake | World |
| Wyrmstake | Load Wyrmstake Blast | Load Wyrmstake Blast | `LT + Y + B` while holding weapon; `LT + B` after certain combos | Consumes all Slinger ammunition to load it into the gunlance and generate a Wyrmstake Blast | Iceborne |
| Wyrmstake | Wyrmstake Blast | Wyrmstake Blast | `Y + B` after Load Wyrmstake Blast; can also be derived from various attacks | Leaves a Wyrmstake Blast on the monster's body; subsequent shell hits will trigger an additional explosion | Iceborne |

## Steps and Evasion Moves

| Category | Move | Reference Name | Common Inputs/Activation Conditions | Description | Version |
|------|--------|-----------------|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------|------|
| Evasion | Backstep | Backstep | `A` while holding weapon | A short-distance step backward | World |
| Evasion | Long Backstep | Backstep (long) | `Back direction + A` after an attack | Covers a greater distance than a normal backstep; cannot immediately follow with another step after completion | World |
| Evasion | Side Step | Side Step | `Left/Right direction + A` after an attack | A short-distance step to the side | World |

## Aerial, Terrain, and Mounted Moves

| Category | Move | Reference Name | Common Inputs/Activation Conditions | Description | Version |
|------|------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|------|
| Aerial | Jumping Thrust | Jumping Thrust | `Y` in mid-air | Thrusts downward; can continue with a horizontal thrust upon landing | World |
| Air | Jumping Smash | Jumping Smash | Air `RT` | Slam the gun downwards in the air; can derive moves like Wide Sweep after landing | World |
| Air | Jumping Reload Smash | Jumping Reload Smash | Air `RT` when ammo is not full | Replenishes Normal Shells during the Jumping Smash | World |
| Air | Aerial Burst | Aerial Burst | Air `B` | Consumes all ammo in mid-air and lands with a powerful Wide Sweep motion | World |
| Air | Aerial Wide Sweep | Aerial Burst (Wide Sweep) | The slashing portion of Aerial Burst | The gun-body wide sweep accompanying Aerial Burst; also recorded as an independent motion segment in the data | World |
| Terrain | Jumping Rising Slash | Jumping Rising Slash | `Y` while sliding down slopes | An upward slash using a slope to leap upward, allowing for further aerial-derived moves | World |
| Terrain | Wall Run Wide Sweep | Wall Run Wide Sweep | Use Lunging Upthrust toward a wall the hunter can run up | A rotational wide sweep after kicking off a wall, which builds mounting gauge | World |
| Mounted | Mounted Finisher (Burst Fire) | Mounted Finisher (Burst Fire) | Press `Y` upon successful mounted offense/defense as prompted on screen | A mounted finisher attack; performs a Full Burst after reloading | World |

## Slinger and Clutch Claw Moves

| Category | Move | Reference Name | Common Input/Activation Conditions | Description | Version |
|--------|----------------|-----------------------------|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|------|
| Slinger | Aim Slinger (Weapon Drawn) | Aim Slinger (Weapon Drawn) | Hold `LT` while weapon is drawn | Directly aims the Slinger while weapon is drawn; also the starting state for loading Wyrmstake Blast | Iceborne |
| Slinger | Fire Slinger (Weapon Drawn) | Fire Slinger (Weapon Drawn) | `RT` while aiming | Fires current Slinger ammunition | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Fire Clutch Claw | Fire Clutch Claw | `B` while aiming | Fires the Clutch Claw and attaches to a large monster's part | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Claw Attack | Claw Attack | `B` after attaching to monster | Changes monster orientation and builds part softening value | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Clutch Claw Weapon Attack | Clutch Claw Weapon Attack | `Y` after attaching to monster | Heavy weapon softening attack for Gunlance; causes part wounds and involves shell reloading/consumption | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Flinch Shot | Flinch Shot | `RT` after attaching to monster's head | Consumes all Slinger ammunition to make a non-enraged monster turn or hit a wall | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Clutch Claw Move | Clutch Claw Move | Push left stick after attaching to monster | Moves between attachable parts of the monster | Iceborne |
| Clutch Claw | Clutch Claw Release | Clutch Claw Release | `A` after attaching to monster | Manually releases the Clutch Claw | Iceborne |
